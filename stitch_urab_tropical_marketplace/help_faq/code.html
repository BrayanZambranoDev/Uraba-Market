Popular Questions

How do I track my organic shipment? keyboard_arrow_down
Once your curated selection departs our facilities, you will receive a botanical journal entry (email) containing a specialized tracking link. You may also trace its journey directly within your Account portal under the 'Heritage Orders' section.
What is your return policy for live goods? keyboard_arrow_down
Due to the delicate nature of living archives, we assess returns on a case-by-case basis. If your specimen arrives in less than optimal condition, please capture a photograph within 24 hours of receipt and contact our specialists for a restorative solution or replacement.
Can I modify my order before it ships? keyboard_arrow_down
Our cultivation process begins swiftly. You have a brief 2-hour window after placing an order to request modifications. Please utilize the 'Urgent Inquiry' function in your order details to reach our fulfillment artisans immediately.
